The track of the Kota Bharu to Emark section of the Ma-East Railway project was officially paved

On March 16, the track of the Kota Bharu to E Mark section of the Malaysian East Coast Railway (Malaysia East Railway) project undertaken by China Communications Construction was officially paved, marking that the track section has entered the stage of system installation, testing and commissioning. The Madong-Malaysia Railway Project is the flagship project of the Belt and Road Initiative between China and Malaysia, with a design length of more than 600 kilometers. Among them, the Kota Bharu to Emark section is the first phase of the project, with a total length of 519 kilometers, which is scheduled to open for operation in January 2027. (Xinhua News Agency).
