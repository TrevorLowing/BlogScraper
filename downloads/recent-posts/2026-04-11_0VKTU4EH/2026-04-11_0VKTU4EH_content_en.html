Power Construction Municipal Contract

Farmland Irrigation Project in Bujumbura Province, Burundi

Recently, PowerChina Municipal Group East Africa Company signed a contract with the Ministry of Environment, Agriculture and Animal Husbandry of Burundi for the LOT1 and LOT2 bid sections of farmland water conservancy improvement projects in three irrigation districts in Bujumbura Province. Jakarta-Bandung high-speed railway has sent more than 15 million passengers

As of April 14, the Jakarta-Bandung high-speed railway, jointly built by China and Indonesia, has sent more than 15 million passengers, with a maximum of 26,770 passengers in a single day and a maximum daily occupancy rate of 99.64%. The Jakarta-Bandung high-speed railway was officially opened on October 17, 2023, with a total length of 142.3 kilometers and a maximum operating speed of 350 kilometers per hour, shortening the travel time from Jakarta to Bandung from more than 3 hours to 46 minutes. (Xinhua News Agency).

China Civil Engineering Contracting

Completion of the Oboroma Bridge project in Bayelsa State, Nigeria

Recently, the completion ceremony of the Oboroma Bridge project in Bayelsa State, Nigeria, undertaken by China Civil Engineering Group Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of China Railway Construction, was held. The Obo-Roman Bridge has a total length of 630 meters, crossing the Nong River, an important tributary of the lower reaches of the Niger River, and adopts a combined structure of prestressed concrete continuous box girder of the main bridge and prestressed T-girder of the approach bridge, with a main span of 75 meters. (China Civil Engineering Group Co., Ltd. public account).
