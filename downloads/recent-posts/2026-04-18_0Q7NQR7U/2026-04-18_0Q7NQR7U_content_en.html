China Architecture won the bid

Etihad High-Speed Rail Civil Engineering and Station Design and Construction Contract Project in the UAE

Recently, China State Construction Middle East Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of China State Construction Group Co., Ltd., joined hands with local UAE companies NPC, Trojan Tunnelling and Turkish company Kalyon to win the bid for the civil engineering and station design and construction project of the Ittihad high-speed railway (P2426 Abu Dhabi section) in the form of a joint venture. The project is located in Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates, and is invested and constructed by Abu Dhabi's state-controlled Ittihad Railways. The project covers the design and construction of about 97 kilometres of double-track high-speed rail and four modern passenger transport hubs. The line is designed to have a maximum speed of 350 km/h, starting from Al Rem Station at the southwest end, passing through core development areas such as Saadiyat Island Station and Yas Island Station, and ending at Abu Dhabi International Airport Station. The scope of the project includes all-round civil construction such as full line subgrade, viaducts, tunnels, large retaining walls, and passenger stations including station buildings and supporting facilities. (China Construction Middle East public account).

China-Laos 500 kV networking project was officially put into operation

On April 20, the commissioning ceremony of the China-Laos 500 kV interconnection project was held simultaneously in Vientiane, the capital of Laos, and Kunming, Yunnan, China. The China-Laos 500 kilovolt networking project is a major project included in the China-Laos community with a shared future action plan, and construction will start in February 2025. The transmission line has a total length of 177.5 kilometers, connecting China's Yunnan Province and Laos' Udom Sai Province and Luang Nan Tha Province, including 145 kilometers in China and 32.5 kilometers in Laos, which are built and operated by China Southern Power Grid Corporation and Lao State Transmission Grid Corporation respectively. (Xinhua News Agency).
