Shanghai Electric Group undertook construction

> the launch ceremony of the 1 GW wind power project in Kazakhstan

On April 9, the launching ceremony of the construction of the 1 GW wind power project in Kazakhstan "Mirny" undertaken by Shanghai Electric Group Co., Ltd. was held in Astana. The project is Kazakhstan's first GW wind power power purchase agreement project, jointly developed by a joint venture company formed by TotalEnergies of France, Samruk Energy of Kazakhstan and Kazakhstan National Oil and Gas Company, with Shanghai Electric Group providing core equipment and overall solutions as the leader of the general contracting consortium. The project has a total construction period of 31 months and is expected to be put into operation in the fourth quarter of 2028, with an annual power generation of 4 billion kWh and an annual reduction of 3.2 million tons of carbon dioxide emissions. (Xinhua News Agency).

China Energy Construction Undertaking

30 MW photovoltaic energy storage power plant in Eritrea was fully put into operation

Recently, the 30 megawatt photovoltaic energy storage project in Deqmhalle, Eritrea, undertaken by China Energy Construction, achieved full capacity grid-connected power generation. The project is located in the Dekembhare region of Eritrea, and is supervised by China Energy Construction Group Shanxi Electric Power Construction Co., Ltd. and China Energy Construction International Construction Group Co., Ltd. The construction of the project covers a 30 megawatt photovoltaic field, a 15 megawatt/30 megawatt-hour energy storage system, a 15/66 kV booster station, and a 750-meter-long dual-circuit 66 kV overhead transmission line. After the project is put into operation, it is expected to generate 73.89 million kWh of electricity per year. (China Energy Construction Co., Ltd. website).
