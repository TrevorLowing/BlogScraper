Manyara Sports Center construction project in Tanzania

Recently, China Road and Bridge Engineering Co., Ltd. won the bid for the construction project of Manyara Sports Center in Tanzania. China Harbor won the bid

Kuala Lumpur-Karat Expressway 1C Extension Project

Recently, China Harbor Engineering Co., Ltd. won the bid for the expansion project of the 1C section of the Kuala Lumpur-Karat Expressway in Malaysia. The 60-kilometer-long project is located between Gombak in Selangor, Malaysia and Karat in Pahang, and includes lane widening, bridge and viaduct expansion, new tunnel construction and related works. (China Harbor Public Account).

Shandong Expressway Group won the bid

Rwanda's road upgrading project

Recently, Shandong Expressway Group International Cooperation Co., Ltd. and Road and Bridge Co., Ltd. cooperated to successfully win the bid for the 55.3-kilometer road upgrading project from Kazabe to Lutsro in Rwanda. The project is located in the western province of Rwanda, and the construction content covers roadbed, pavement, drainage, traffic safety facilities and other projects, with a construction period of 36 months. (Shandong Expressway Group public account).

China Communications Construction Contract

The three bid project of the Mojo-Hawasa Expressway in Ethiopia successfully passed the preliminary inspection

Recently, the third bid project of the Mojo-Hawasa Expressway in Ethiopia undertaken by China Communications Construction successfully passed the initial acceptance and obtained the preliminary verification certificate. Power China undertook construction

The Eli Pumping Station of the Ambatale Water Plant Efficiency Improvement Project in Sri Lanka was successfully handed over

Recently, the efficiency improvement and renovation project of Ambatale Water Plant in Sri Lanka undertaken by PowerChina held a handover ceremony for the Ely pumping station. The project is located in Colombo, the capital of Sri Lanka, and a total of 4 pumps are installed in the pumping room, with a single pump head of 40 meters, a flow rate of 2,400 cubic meters per hour, and a daily processing capacity of 150,000 tons.
